## Biography

<!-- Replace the sample line with a paragraph about your background,
     interests, or what you are working on. -->

He is a student at Assumption College in Bangkok and president of SPACE AC, the school’s aerospace club. His interests include computing, embedded systems, control systems, and avionics. He led Team DAEDALUS to first place at the American Astronautical Society’s 2026 CanSat Competition in Virginia and helped develop its onboard flight computer and autonomous flight control software. 

In 2025, he was a programmer on Team Lorentz, which placed second in its remote-control division and won the Task Challenge Award at NASA’s Human Exploration Rover Challenge. He also designed circuit boards for the Passenger VI–IX high-altitude balloon missions and helped organize the 2025 AC × KMUTT Rocket Camp as Head of Avionics.
